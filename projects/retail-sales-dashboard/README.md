# 🧮 Retail Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## 📘 Project Overview
This Excel dashboard analyzes retail sales performance across regions, product categories, and time periods.  
It was built as part of my IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate.

## 🧠 Key Insights
- The North and East regions generate the highest total sales and profit, while the West region also performs strongly in revenue.
- The Technology category dominates performance, contributing over 70% of total sales and around 69% of total profit, whereas Office Supplies has the smallest share.
- Sales show noticeable peaks in April 2024 and mid-2025, indicating strong seasonal demand or promotional periods.
- Headsets and Monitors are the top-selling products, together accounting for a major portion of total sales.
- The overall Profit Margin is 20.35%, reflecting healthy profitability across all analyzed categories.
